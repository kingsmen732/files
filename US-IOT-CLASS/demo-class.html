THE SECRET

How does it know what colour? 🤔

🖥️
Step 1 — Prismatik watches the screen
Software looks at the edges of your monitor 30 times per second
→
🎨
Step 2 — Calculates zone colours
Averages the colour in each zone — left edge, right edge, top, bottom
→
📡
Step 3 — Sends colour data over WiFi
Colour packets fly across the home network to the ESP32
→
💡
Step 4 — LEDs light up the right colour!
Each LED behind the monitor matches the zone it sits in
DONE ✓

⚡ All 4 steps happen in under 50 milliseconds — you never see a delay!
